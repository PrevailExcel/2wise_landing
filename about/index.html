About 2Wise

2Wise is JAMB practice that finds where you're losing marks and fixes it. It's built in Aba and tested with real JAMB students.

2Wise is an independent JAMB UTME practice app for Nigerian students, made by Reevatech Solutions & Consulting Ltd in Aba. It was built by Prevail Ejimadu, a solutions architect and General Manager of Eilit Hub, Aba. 2Wise is not affiliated with JAMB.

Why we built 2Wise

Working with JAMB students at Eilit Hub, we kept seeing the same thing: hardworking students who read for hours, did past questions with the answers showing, and still scored far below what they were capable of. The problem wasn't effort. It was that nobody showed them which topics were costing them marks, and they had never practised the exam the way it's actually written, on a screen and against the clock.

2Wise does those two things. It gives you the real CBT format, 180 questions in 2 hours scored out of 400, and it tracks every answer so it can tell you exactly which topics to fix next.

Who builds 2Wise

Prevail Ejimadu is a solutions architect who designs and builds software products across education, fintech and SaaS, and has led engineering teams from idea to launch. He is also General Manager of Eilit Hub in Aba, a training hub for tech skills, exam preparation and professional courses. Students at Eilit Hub use 2Wise, and their feedback shapes what we build next.

How we write our guides

  • We check every JAMB date, fee and rule against JAMB's own announcements or reliable Nigerian news sources, and we link to our sources at the bottom of each guide.
  • When JAMB hasn't announced something yet, we say so plainly instead of guessing.
  • We update guides when JAMB changes things, and show the date each one was last checked.
  • We don't publish fake reviews, invented statistics or "expo".

Contact

Email hello@2wise.com.ng. For official JAMB matters, always contact JAMB directly through jamb.gov.ng.
