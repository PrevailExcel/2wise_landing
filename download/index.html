How to install

    All platforms

    Pick your device

    Android

    Phones and tablets

    The full app: offline practice, daily reminders, Share on WhatsApp, and battle links that open straight in the app.

    Download APK
    How to install
    1. Tap Download APK. When it finishes, open the file from your notifications or Downloads.
    2. If asked, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
    3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway. That's only because we're not on the Play Store yet.
    4. Open 2Wise, sign in with Google, and tap Allow for notifications so you get your daily words.

    Google Play listing coming soon

    Windows

    Windows 10 and 11

    A proper desktop app for long study sessions and full mocks on a bigger screen. Works offline.

    Download for Windows
    How to install
    1. Click Download for Windows and open 2wise-setup.exe when it finishes.
    2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
    3. Follow the installer. 2Wise appears in your Start menu.
    4. Open 2Wise and sign in with Google. Your browser opens for sign-in, then sends you back to the app.

    Linux

    AppImage or .deb

    Same desktop app for Ubuntu, Linux Mint, Debian and most other distributions.

    AppImage .deb
    How to install
    1. AppImage (any distro): right-click the file, open Properties → Permissions, tick Allow executing as program, then double-click it. Or run chmod +x 2wise.AppImage && ./2wise.AppImage.
    2. .deb (Ubuntu, Mint, Debian): double-click to open in your software installer, or run sudo apt install ./2wise.deb.
    3. Open 2Wise and sign in with Google.

    iPhone & iPad

    Web app in Safari

    Add 2Wise to your Home Screen and it opens full-screen like any other app, and works offline.

    Open in Safari
    How to add it
    1. Open app.2wise.com.ng in Safari (not Chrome).
    2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen , then Add.
    4. Open 2Wise from your Home Screen and sign in with Google.

    Mac

    Web app

    Use 2Wise in Safari or Chrome, or add it to your Dock so it opens in its own window.

    Open 2Wise
    How to add it to your Dock
    1. Open app.2wise.com.ng in Safari.
    2. Choose File → Add to Dock (macOS Sonoma or later). In Chrome, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
    3. Open 2Wise from your Dock and sign in with Google.

    Questions

    Installing 2Wise

    Is 2Wise free to download?

    Yes. The app is free on every platform, and your first 30 days include everything in Premium. After that Premium is ₦1,000 for 12 months.

    Is 2Wise on the Google Play Store?

    Not yet. The Play Store listing is coming soon. Until then, download the Android app directly from this page. It's the same app.

    Why does my phone or computer warn me about installing 2Wise?

    Android shows a Play Protect notice, and Windows may show SmartScreen, for apps installed from outside an app store. It's expected while we wait for store listings. Tap More details then Install anyway (Android), or More info then Run anyway (Windows).

    Can I use 2Wise on iPhone or Mac?

    Yes, in the browser. Open app.2wise.com.ng in Safari. On iPhone or iPad tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on a Mac use File, then Add to Dock. It opens like an app and works offline.

    Will my progress move between devices?

    Yes. Sign in with the same Google account on any device and your mocks, streak, words and Premium come with you.
