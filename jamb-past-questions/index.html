Does JAMB repeat past questions?

Rarely word for word, but JAMB tests the same syllabus topics and the same question patterns every year. That's why past questions work: once you've seen enough questions on a topic, the new version in your exam feels familiar. The trick is to practise them timed and fix the topic behind every wrong answer, not to memorise answers. Our guide to using JAMB past questions explains the full method.

Does JAMB repeat past questions?

Around ten years per subject is a good target, but how you practise matters more: timed, marked honestly, with every wrong answer traced back to a topic you then fix.
