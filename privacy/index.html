Privacy policy

This policy explains what information 2Wise collects, why, and the choices you have. Last updated 27 November 2026.

2Wise collects only what it needs to run your account and your practice: your name, email, the exam and subjects you choose, your answers and progress, and, if you choose to give it, your phone number. We don't sell your information. Payments are handled by Paystack, and we never see or store your card details.

Who we are

2Wise is operated by Reevatech Solutions & Consulting Ltd, Aba, Nigeria ("2Wise", "we"). Contact: hello@2wise.com.ng.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, from Google sign-in or the details you enter.
  • Study details: your exam, subjects, target score, exam date, and your answers, scores and progress.
  • Phone number (optional): if you add it, so we can reach you on WhatsApp with study updates and offers. You can leave it out.
  • Payments: Paystack processes payments. We receive confirmation that you paid and the plan you bought, not your card or bank details.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information such as device type, app version and crash reports, and a push-notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Website analytics: our website uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand visits and measure our ads.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and sync your progress across devices.
  • To personalise your practice, for example by finding your weak topics.
  • To answer questions you ask the AI tutor. The question and the relevant practice question are sent to our AI provider to generate an answer.
  • To send notifications and, if you gave your number, WhatsApp messages you can opt out of at any time.
  • To process payments, prevent abuse and improve 2Wise.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run 2Wise (hosting, payments through Paystack, analytics, notifications and AI providers), and only for those purposes, or when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop WhatsApp messages, by emailing hello@2wise.com.ng. We handle requests in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except where we must keep records for legal or payment reasons.

Students under 18

Many JAMB candidates are under 18. 2Wise is a study tool and collects only what it needs to provide practice. Parents or guardians can contact us at any time about a student's account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
