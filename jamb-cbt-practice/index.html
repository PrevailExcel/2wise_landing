Free JAMB CBT practice for 2027

The UTME is a computer-based test. The fastest way to raise your score is to practise on a screen, against the clock, the way the real exam works. Here's what the JAMB CBT looks like and how to practise it for free.

PEPrevail EjimaduSolutions Architect · GM, Eilit Hub · Updated 27 November 2026

JAMB's UTME is a computer-based test (CBT): Use of English plus three subjects, 180 questions in 2 hours, each subject scored out of 100 for a total of 400. 2Wise gives you the same format as a free JAMB CBT practice app: full timed mocks with a question navigator, 20,000+ JAMB past questions, and an explanation for every answer. It runs on Android, Windows, Linux and in any browser, and works offline once your questions download.

Many candidates prepare for JAMB on paper, then meet the CBT screen for the first time on exam day. They lose minutes working out the navigator, scrolling through passages and checking the clock. Those minutes are marks. CBT practice removes that shock before it costs you.

What does the JAMB CBT look like?

  • Four subjects, one sitting. Use of English (60 questions) and your three other subjects (40 questions each): 180 questions in total.
  • Two hours for everything. The time is shared across all four subjects, and you can move between subjects freely.
  • A question navigator showing which questions you've answered, so you can jump back to ones you skipped.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: A, B, C and D to choose options, and N and P for next and previous question.
  • An on-screen calculator for calculation questions.
  • Scored out of 400. There is no negative marking, so an unanswered question is simply a lost mark. See how JAMB is scored.

Why practise JAMB on a CBT app instead of on paper?

Paper practice teaches you the content. CBT practice teaches you the exam. On a screen you learn your real pace (about 40 seconds a question on average), how long comprehension passages take you to scroll through, and when to skip and come back. A CBT practice app also marks you instantly and keeps a record of which topics you keep missing, which a past-question book can't do.

How to use CBT practice to raise your score

  1. Take one full mock now to get your starting score out of 400.
  2. Practise your weakest topics daily, 40–60 questions a day, reviewing every wrong answer.
  3. Take one full timed mock a week from about ten weeks before the exam.
  4. Practise the two-pass method: answer everything you know first, then come back for the hard ones. Our CBT exam-day tips explain it.
