JAMB 2027: everything you need to know

What to expect from the 2027 UTME: the exam format, how to get your profile code, typical registration timing and fees (based on 2026), the set novel, and how to start preparing now.

PEPrevail EjimaduFounder of 2Wise · JAMB tutor at Eilit Hub, Aba · Updated 22 October 2026 · 3 min read

The 2027 UTME is expected to follow the usual format: a computer-based test in Use of English plus three subjects, 180 questions in 2 hours, scored out of 400. For reference, 2026 registration ran from 26 January to 28 February 2026, with the main exam from 16 to 25 April 2026 and fees of ₦7,200 (₦8,700 with the optional mock). JAMB announces the 2027 dates and fees itself, so confirm everything on jamb.gov.ng.

JAMB 2027 is closer than it feels. Candidates who start preparing now, not when registration opens, have months of advantage. Here's what you can expect, based on how the 2026 UTME ran.

What is the JAMB 2027 exam format?

  • Computer-based test (CBT) at an accredited centre
  • Four subjects: Use of English (compulsory) plus three related to your course (see subject combinations)
  • 180 questions: 60 in English and 40 in each other subject
  • 2 hours in total
  • Scored out of 400: 100 per subject (how scoring works)

How do I register for JAMB?

  1. Get your NIN if you don't have one. Registration is tied to your National Identification Number.
  2. Get your profile code: in 2026 this meant texting "NIN" followed by your 11-digit NIN to 55019 or 66019.
  3. Buy your e-PIN through an approved channel.
  4. Register at an accredited CBT centre, with your subject combination and institution choices ready.
  5. Print your exam slip when printing opens, about one to two weeks before the exam.
Tips from the 2026 rules: use one personal phone number throughout, never create a second profile with another NIN, register only at approved centres, and keep henna off your fingers for biometric capture.

What novel will JAMB use in 2027?

The set novel for the 2026 UTME was The Lekki Headmaster by Kabir Alabi Garba. JAMB confirms the text with each registration, but if you're preparing now, start with this one. Here's how to study The Lekki Headmaster.

What score will I need?

For 2026, JAMB's minimum was 150 for universities and 100 for polytechnics, but competitive courses need far more. See JAMB cut-off marks explained.

How should I start preparing for JAMB 2027?

  1. Confirm your subject combination
  2. Sit a full timed mock to get your baseline score out of 400
  3. Build a daily routine around your weakest topics
  4. Learn five English words a day and read the novel
  5. Sit one full mock a week and track your score

Frequently asked questions

When will JAMB 2027 registration start?

JAMB hasn't announced it yet. For reference, 2026 registration ran from 26 January to 28 February 2026, so registration for 2027 is likely to open early in 2027. Watch jamb.gov.ng for the official dates.

How much is the JAMB 2027 form?

JAMB announces fees with each registration. In 2026 the UTME cost ₦7,200 without the mock, or ₦8,700 with the optional mock exam.

How do I get my JAMB profile code?

In 2026, candidates sent their 11-digit NIN by SMS in the format NIN followed by the number to 55019 or 66019 and received a 10-character profile code. Use one personal phone number for the whole process.

What is the minimum age for JAMB?

In 2026, candidates had to be at least 16 by 30 September of the exam year. Check the 2027 registration notice for the current rule.

Is the JAMB mock exam compulsory?

No, the JAMB mock UTME is optional and costs extra. It's a useful dress rehearsal, but you can practise the full format at home anytime with a CBT mock app.

Last checked 22 October 2026. JAMB changes dates and rules from year to year. Always confirm on jamb.gov.ng.
