WAEC 2027: exam date, registration and the new hybrid CBT mode

WAEC has opened registration for the 2027 WASSCE for school candidates and says the exam will run in hybrid mode. Here's the proposed date, what hybrid CBT means for you, what's happening with fees, and how to prepare for WAEC and JAMB together.

PEPrevail EjimaduFounder of 2Wise · JAMB tutor at Eilit Hub, Aba · Updated 27 November 2026

The 2027 WASSCE for school candidates is proposed for 11 May – 18 June 2027 under the harmonised national exam timetable, after the UTME (15–25 March) and alongside NECO (1 April – 14 May). WAEC opened registration for school candidates in November 2026 through schools, and says the 2027 exam will be conducted in hybrid mode: objective questions on computer, essays and practicals on paper. A proposed rise in the fee to ₦50,000 was suspended.

If you're in SS3, 2027 is a heavy year: JAMB in March, then NECO and WAEC from April to June. The good news is that the three exams test a lot of the same syllabus. Plan them together and each one makes the next easier.

When is WAEC 2027?

Under the harmonised 2027 exam timetable shared in October 2026, the proposed dates are:

Exam (2027)Proposed dates
JAMB UTME15 – 25 March 2027
NECO SSCE1 April – 14 May 2027
WAEC WASSCE (school candidates)11 May – 18 June 2027
NABTEB1 June – 5 July 2027

These dates are provisional. WAEC will publish the subject-by-subject timetable closer to the exam. Read more on the 2027 exam timetable.

Has WAEC 2027 registration started?

Yes, for school candidates. In November 2026 WAEC announced that registration for the 2027 WASSCE for school candidates was open, and urged schools to submit candidates' details and upload continuous assessment scores within its deadlines. School candidates register through their schools, so ask your school for its internal deadline and make sure your name, date of birth and subjects are correct before submission.

WAEC's guidance for 2026 and 2027 candidates is to register a minimum of 8 and a maximum of 9 subjects, with English Language and General Mathematics as core subjects. Confirm your combination with your school.

What does WAEC's hybrid CBT mode mean?

WAEC has been moving to computer-based testing. In the hybrid model it first used for private candidates, objective (multiple-choice) questions are answered on a computer, while essay and practical papers are still written by hand. WAEC says the 2027 school exam will run in hybrid mode, and it has talked about a practice portal so schools and candidates can get used to the system.

For you, that means two things. You need to be comfortable answering objective questions on a screen, against a timer. And you still need to practise writing full essay and theory answers by hand.

Good news if you're also writing JAMB: the UTME is fully CBT, so every hour of JAMB CBT practice also prepares you for WAEC's objective papers.

How much is WAEC 2027 registration?

In mid-2026 the Federal Ministry of Education approved raising the WAEC and NECO school-candidate fee to ₦50,000 from 2027, up from about ₦27,000. After public outcry, the government suspended the increase. Ask your school for the fee it's collecting, and be wary of anyone charging "special centre" or "assistance" fees.

How should I prepare for WAEC and JAMB together?

  1. Until mid-March: JAMB first. Focus on your four UTME subjects with timed CBT practice. Most of that syllabus overlaps with WAEC.
  2. Keep writing. Once a week, write one full theory or essay answer by hand in an SS3 subject, so the skill doesn't fade while you do objectives.
  3. After the UTME: switch to WAEC mode. Add your other WAEC subjects, past essay questions and practicals.
  4. Use past questions properly. Attempt them timed, mark yourself and fix the topic behind every wrong answer. Our past questions method works for WAEC objectives too.
