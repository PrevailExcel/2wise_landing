JAMB syllabus 2027: the topics to study in every major subject

The JAMB syllabus is the official list of everything the UTME can test. Here's what's in the 2027 JAMB syllabus for Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Government and Literature, and how to turn it into a study plan.

PEPrevail EjimaduFounder of 2Wise · JAMB tutor at Eilit Hub, Aba · Updated 23 November 2026 · 8 min read

The JAMB syllabus lists every topic the UTME can test in each subject, and JAMB's questions are drawn only from it. JAMB has not announced a new syllabus for 2027, so the current UTME syllabus on jamb.gov.ng is the one to study. Use it as a checklist: tick off each topic, practise past questions on it, and spend most of your time on the topics you score lowest in.

Most JAMB candidates study from a textbook, front to back. The problem is that textbooks contain far more than JAMB tests, and sometimes skip things JAMB does test. The JAMB syllabus fixes that. It's the exact list of topics the UTME can ask about, and it's free.

What is the JAMB syllabus?

The JAMB syllabus is an official document for each UTME subject. For every subject, it sets out the general objectives, the topics and sub-topics you're expected to know, and what you should be able to do with each one (define, calculate, explain, compare). Every question in the UTME is meant to come from these topics.

You can read the official JAMB syllabus for every subject on jamb.gov.ng. Many websites copy it; if a copy disagrees with JAMB's site, trust JAMB.

Is there a new JAMB syllabus for 2027?

As of 23 November 2026, JAMB has not announced a new syllabus for the 2027 UTME. The syllabus changes rarely, so the current one is what you should study for JAMB 2027. The parts that do change more often are the Use of English set novel and the Literature-in-English prescribed texts. Check those when JAMB opens 2027 registration.

Remember the exam is earlier this time: the 2027 UTME is proposed for 15–25 March 2027. That makes a syllabus-first plan even more important. You don't have time to study topics JAMB won't ask.

JAMB syllabus for Use of English

Use of English is compulsory for every candidate and has 60 questions. The syllabus covers three areas:

SectionWhat it covers
Comprehension and summaryReading passages (description, narration, exposition, argument), finding main ideas, inference, tone and purpose, and questions on the prescribed reading text (the set novel)
Lexis and structureSynonyms, antonyms, sentence completion, sentence interpretation, idioms and figurative expressions, concord, tenses, word classes, spelling and punctuation
Oral formsVowels, consonants, rhymes, word stress and emphatic stress, about 10 questions in total

The set novel for the 2025 and 2026 UTME was The Lekki Headmaster; JAMB hasn't named the 2027 text yet. For lexis, see how to stop losing easy synonym and antonym marks.

JAMB syllabus for Mathematics

The JAMB Mathematics syllabus is divided into five sections:

  • Number and numeration: number bases, fractions, decimals, approximations and percentages, indices, logarithms and surds, sets
  • Algebra: polynomials, variation, inequalities, progressions (arithmetic and geometric), binary operations, matrices and determinants
  • Geometry and trigonometry: Euclidean geometry, mensuration, loci, coordinate geometry, trigonometry
  • Calculus: differentiation and its applications, integration and its applications
  • Statistics: representation of data, measures of location and dispersion, permutation and combination, probability

JAMB syllabus for Physics

The Physics syllabus runs from mechanics to modern physics. The main topics are:

  • Mechanics: measurements and units, scalars and vectors, motion, gravitational field, equilibrium of forces, work, energy and power, friction, simple machines, elasticity, pressure, liquids at rest
  • Heat: temperature and its measurement, thermal expansion, gas laws, quantity of heat, change of state, vapours, structure of matter and kinetic theory, heat transfer
  • Waves and light: waves, propagation of sound, light energy, reflection, refraction, optical instruments, dispersion of light and colours
  • Electricity and magnetism: electrostatics, capacitors, electric cells, current electricity, electrical energy and power, magnets and magnetic fields, force on a current-carrying conductor, electromagnetic induction, simple a.c. circuits, conduction of electricity through liquids and gases
  • Modern physics: elementary modern physics and introductory electronics

JAMB syllabus for Chemistry

  • Foundations: separation of mixtures and purification, chemical combination (laws, the mole, stoichiometry), kinetic theory of matter and gas laws, atomic structure and bonding
  • The environment: air, water, solubility, environmental pollution
  • Reactions: acids, bases and salts, oxidation and reduction, electrolysis, energy changes, rates of chemical reaction, chemical equilibria
  • Elements: non-metals and their compounds, metals and their compounds
  • Organic chemistry and chemistry and industry

JAMB syllabus for Biology

The Biology syllabus has five big sections:

  • Variety of organisms: living and non-living things, classification, cell structure and organisation, and the organisms in each kingdom
  • Form and function: internal structure of plants and animals, nutrition, transport, respiration, excretion, support and movement, reproduction, growth, coordination and control
  • Ecology: factors affecting living things, ecosystems and food chains, adaptation, pollution and conservation
  • Heredity and variation: genetics, variation and its application
  • Evolution: theories of evolution and evidence for it

SubjectMain areas in the JAMB syllabus

GovernmentElements of government (state, sovereignty, democracy, constitutions, arms of government, federalism, electoral process, political parties and pressure groups), political development in Nigeria, public administration, international relations and organisations
Literature-in-EnglishDrama, prose and poetry (African and non-African), general literary principles and devices, literary appreciation of unseen extracts, and the prescribed texts for the year
CommerceOccupation, production, trade (home and foreign), aids to trade, business units, financing business, the stock exchange, business management, consumerism and government's role in business

For CRS, IRS, Geography, Agricultural Science, the Nigerian languages and other subjects, read the full syllabus for your subject on jamb.gov.ng. Not sure which four subjects you need? Check JAMB subject combinations for popular courses.

How should you use the JAMB syllabus to study?

  1. Print or save the syllabus for your four subjects. Turn each topic into a line on a checklist.
  2. Rate every topic: green (I can answer questions on this), amber (shaky), red (I don't know it).
  3. Sit a full mock to check your ratings against reality. Students often think a topic is green until a timed question proves otherwise.
  4. Study red topics first, then amber. For each topic, read it briefly, then go straight to past questions on that topic.
  5. Re-rate weekly. When a topic turns green, it only needs light revision until the exam.
Don't treat every topic as equal. Some syllabus topics produce several questions every year, others appear rarely. Past questions show you which is which, so let them decide where your hours go.

Frequently asked questions

Is the JAMB syllabus for 2027 out?

JAMB has not announced a new syllabus for 2027. The current UTME syllabus on jamb.gov.ng is the one to study. The set novel and Literature prescribed texts are the parts that change most often, so check those when 2027 registration opens.

Where can I download the official JAMB syllabus?

The official syllabus for every UTME subject is on JAMB's website, jamb.gov.ng. If a copy on another site disagrees with JAMB's, trust JAMB's version.

Does JAMB set questions outside the syllabus?

JAMB's questions are meant to come from its syllabus. A question can feel like it's outside the syllabus when it tests a sub-topic in an unfamiliar way, which is why past-question practice matters alongside the syllabus.

How many questions are in the JAMB Use of English syllabus?

Use of English has 60 questions in the UTME, covering comprehension and summary (including the set novel), lexis and structure, and oral forms. Every other subject has 40 questions.

What is the best way to study the JAMB syllabus?

Turn it into a checklist, rate each topic by how well you know it, take a full mock to confirm, then study your weakest topics first with past questions on each one. Re-rate your topics every week.

Last checked 23 November 2026. JAMB changes dates and rules from year to year. Always confirm on jamb.gov.ng.
