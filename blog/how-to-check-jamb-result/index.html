How to check your JAMB result by SMS and online

JAMB results usually come out within days of the exam. Here's exactly how to check yours by SMS and on the JAMB portal, what each reply means, how to print your result slip, and which 'result upgrade' offers to ignore.

PEPrevail EjimaduSolutions Architect · GM, Eilit Hub · Updated 27 November 2026

To check your JAMB result, send UTMERESULT by SMS to 55019 or 66019 from the same phone number (SIM) you used to register. You need airtime: the service cost about ₦100 per check in 2026. You can also view your score on JAMB's e-Facility portal, and print your official result slip there once JAMB switches printing on. The 2027 UTME is proposed for 15–25 March 2027, so expect results from late March.

The days after the UTME are tense. The good news is that checking your result takes less than a minute, if you use the right method. Here's everything, based on how JAMB ran result checking in 2026.

How do I check my JAMB result by SMS?

  1. Use the phone and SIM you registered with. Results are tied to that number.
  2. Make sure you have airtime. In 2026 each check cost about ₦100 (some reports said it rose from ₦50).
  3. Type UTMERESULT in capital letters, as one word.
  4. Send it to 55019 or 66019.
  5. Wait for JAMB's reply with your score in each subject and your total out of 400.

The same method works for the mock: in 2026 candidates sent MOCKRESULT to the same numbers.

Forgot your registration number? In 2026 JAMB's retrieval service worked by texting RETRIEVE to 55019 from your registered number. Check JAMB's current instructions before you rely on it.

How do I check my JAMB result online?

  1. Go to JAMB's official e-Facility portal through jamb.gov.ng.
  2. Log in with the email address and password you created during registration.
  3. Open the result-checking option, select the exam year and enter your registration number.

In 2026, SMS checking opened first and the online slip printing came later in the release cycle, so don't panic if the portal isn't ready on day one.

How do I print my JAMB result slip?

Your SMS reply shows your score, but universities ask for the official result slip with your photograph. In 2026 JAMB opened slip printing through its portal for a fee of about ₦1,500. Print it only from JAMB's official website, and keep a few copies for Post-UTME screening.

What does my JAMB result SMS mean?

ReplyWhat it means
Your scores per subject and totalYour result is out. Save the message and print your slip when printing opens.
Result not yet released / no result yetYour result is still being processed. Check again later; results come out in batches.
Result withheldJAMB is investigating something about your exam. Follow JAMB's instructions and contact JAMB through official channels only.
Holding pending documentsJAMB needs something from you, often for underage or special cases. Upload what's requested on the portal.
You did not register / wrong numberYou're probably texting from a different SIM. Use the number you registered with.

Can anyone upgrade my JAMB score?

No. Anyone offering to "upgrade" your JAMB score or "unlock" a withheld result for a fee is a scammer. JAMB has repeatedly warned candidates about this. Use only jamb.gov.ng and JAMB's verified social media accounts.

What should I do after checking my JAMB result?

  • Work out your aggregate with our JAMB aggregate calculator, then compare it with your course's cut-off. See JAMB cut-off marks.
  • Prepare for Post-UTME if your school holds one. The same past-question practice that works for JAMB works for Post-UTME.
  • Not happy with your score? A low score usually comes from a few fixable causes. Read how to score much higher on a retake.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my JAMB result with my phone?

Send UTMERESULT by SMS to 55019 or 66019 from the phone number you used for JAMB registration. You need airtime; it cost about ₦100 per check in 2026.

When will the JAMB 2027 result be released?

The 2027 UTME is proposed for 15–25 March 2027. In 2026, JAMB released the first results within days of the exam, so expect 2027 results from late March, but wait for JAMB's announcement.

Can I check my JAMB result without my registration number?

For the SMS method you only need the phone number you registered with. If you've lost your registration number, JAMB's retrieval SMS service (RETRIEVE to 55019 in 2026) sends it to your registered phone.

How much is JAMB result slip printing?

In 2026 JAMB's official result slip printing cost about ₦1,500 on its portal. Print only from JAMB's official website.

Why does my JAMB result say withheld?

A withheld result means JAMB is reviewing something about your exam. Follow the instructions JAMB gives, contact it only through official channels, and ignore anyone who offers to release it for money.

Last checked 27 November 2026. JAMB changes dates and rules from year to year. Always confirm on jamb.gov.ng.
