JAMB 2027 date: the UTME moves to March 15–25, 2027

The 2027 UTME has been proposed for 15–25 March 2027, about a month earlier than usual. Here's the full 2027 exam timetable, what it means for JAMB registration and the mock, the new rules for 2027, and a 16-week plan to get ready.

PEPrevail EjimaduFounder of 2Wise · JAMB tutor at Eilit Hub, Aba · Updated 23 November 2026 · 6 min read

The 2027 UTME is proposed to hold from 15 to 25 March 2027, under a harmonised national exam timetable that JAMB shared in October 2026. That's about a month earlier than the April dates of recent years. JAMB says the dates are provisional, and it hasn't announced the 2027 registration dates, fees, mock date or novel yet. If the March date holds, you have roughly 16 weeks from today to prepare.

If you were planning your JAMB 2027 preparation around an April exam, change the plan now. The Federal Government has proposed a harmonised timetable for all the big 2027 exams, and the UTME comes first, in March. A month less to prepare is a month less to fix your weak topics, so here's everything we know about the JAMB 2027 date so far, and what to do about it.

When is JAMB 2027?

The proposed date for the 2027 Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) is 15–25 March 2027. JAMB published the harmonised 2027 timetable on its social media pages in October 2026 and said the dates are provisional, subject to confirmation by each exam body.

Exam (2027)Proposed dates
JAMB UTME15 – 25 March 2027
NECO SSCE1 April – 14 May 2027
WAEC WASSCE11 May – 18 June 2027
NABTEB1 June – 5 July 2027
NBAIS10 June – 9 July 2027

For comparison, the 2026 UTME ran from 16 April 2026. So the JAMB 2027 exam date is about four weeks earlier than candidates are used to.

Why did JAMB move the UTME to March?

The goal of the harmonised timetable is to stop exams clashing and to get results out in time for admission. The plan is for the major exam bodies to release results by August 2027, so that eligible candidates can start tertiary admission processes by September 2027.

For you, the practical effect is simple: the UTME now comes before your NECO and WAEC exams, not in the middle of them. If you're in SS3, you'll write JAMB first and then switch to WAEC and NECO preparation.

When will JAMB 2027 registration start?

JAMB has not announced the 2027 UTME registration date yet. As of today, it has released preparation advice and a checklist, but no opening or closing date for registration and no 2027 e-PIN prices.

For reference, here's how the 2026 cycle ran:

Stage (2026)Dates
e-PIN sales19 January – 26 February 2026
Registration26 January – 28 February 2026
Optional mock UTME28 March 2026
Main UTME16 April 2026

In 2026 registration closed about seven weeks before the exam. With the exam moving forward to mid-March, it's reasonable to expect JAMB 2027 registration to open earlier than late January, but that's our expectation, not an announcement. Watch jamb.gov.ng and JAMB's verified X account, @jamb_hq.

The 2026 fees were ₦7,200 for the UTME without mock, ₦8,700 with the mock and ₦5,700 for Direct Entry. JAMB has said candidates shouldn't assume those figures will apply in 2027.

When is the JAMB 2027 mock exam?

Not announced yet. In 2026 the optional mock UTME held on 28 March, about three weeks before the main exam. If JAMB keeps a similar gap, the 2027 mock would fall in late February 2027. Treat that as a guess until JAMB confirms it.

What's new for the 2027 UTME?

JAMB has a new Registrar, Professor Segun Aina, a Computer Engineering professor from OAU, who took over from Professor Is-haq Oloyede after Oloyede's tenure ended on 31 July 2026. Several changes have been announced for the 2027 UTME so far:

  • A JAMB pre-registration app. Announced on 14 November 2026, it should let you start registration from home: pull your details from NIMC, fill in your biodata, and choose your subjects, courses and institutions. It's also meant to show previous-year entry marks for courses. You'll still finish registration and biometric capture at an accredited CBT centre. No download link or launch date has been published yet, so ignore anyone selling you "the JAMB app" before JAMB announces it.
  • Wrong subject combinations blocked. The system is meant to stop you if your four UTME subjects don't match your course. Check yours early in our JAMB subject combinations guide and on JAMB's IBASS portal.
  • A "report this question" button on the CBT screen, so candidates can flag questions they believe are faulty. JAMB has warned that a hard question isn't the same as a faulty one.
  • CBT centre ratings after the exam, and stricter biometric checks at centres.
  • New arrival time. For the first session of the 2027 UTME, candidates should arrive by 7:30 a.m. instead of 6:30 a.m. That exam runs 8:30–10:30 a.m.
  • No UTME for some Colleges of Education courses. From the 2027 admission cycle, candidates for education and non-engineering agriculture programmes at Colleges of Education are exempt from the UTME.
  • NIN alignment reopened. JAMB reopened its NIN alignment service on 16 October 2026 and described it as a one-time opportunity. If your NIN details don't match your records, fix them with NIMC first.

What does a March UTME mean for your preparation?

From today (23 November 2026) to 15 March 2027 is about 16 weeks. That's enough time to move your score a long way, if you use it on the right things. Here's how we'd split it:

WeeksFocus
1 – 2Confirm your subject combination. Sit one full, timed mock to get your baseline score out of 400. List your weakest topics in each subject.
3 – 10Topic-by-topic practice on your weak areas, every day. Five new English words a day. Read the set novel once JAMB confirms it.
11 – 14One full mock every week, under exam conditions. Review every wrong answer the same day.
15 – 16Exam mode: timed sections, past questions on your weakest topics, sleep and routine. No new big topics.
The biggest risk with a March exam is the December holidays. Christmas and New Year will take two weeks out of your 16 if you let them. Plan short daily sessions through the holidays instead of stopping completely.

What should you do this week?

JAMB's own advice to 2027 candidates lists five avoidable mistakes. Turn them into this week's to-do list:

  1. Start studying now, not when registration opens. Make a realistic timetable.
  2. Check your course requirements, including the O'level credits your chosen school needs.
  3. Confirm your subject combination on IBASS before you register.
  4. Follow only official channels: jamb.gov.ng and JAMB's verified social media accounts.
  5. Check your personal details: name spelling, date of birth, NIN and phone number must match your official records.

Then sit one full mock. Knowing your real starting score turns "I need to read everything" into a short, specific list of topics to fix.

Frequently asked questions

When is JAMB 2027?

The 2027 UTME is proposed for 15–25 March 2027, according to the harmonised 2027 exam timetable JAMB shared in October 2026. JAMB says the dates are provisional until officially confirmed.

Has JAMB 2027 registration started?

No. As of 23 November 2026, JAMB has not announced the 2027 UTME registration dates or fees. In 2026 registration ran from 26 January to 28 February. Watch jamb.gov.ng and @jamb_hq for the official dates.

When is the JAMB 2027 mock exam?

JAMB hasn't announced it. In 2026 the optional mock held on 28 March, about three weeks before the main UTME. With the 2027 exam moving to March, expect the mock date to move earlier too.

What time should I arrive for the 2027 UTME?

For the first session of the 2027 UTME, JAMB says candidates should arrive at their CBT centre by 7:30 a.m., instead of the old 6:30 a.m. That session runs from 8:30 to 10:30 a.m.

Who is the JAMB Registrar for 2027?

Professor Segun Aina, appointed by President Tinubu in May 2026. He succeeded Professor Is-haq Oloyede, whose tenure ended on 31 July 2026.

What is the JAMB 2027 novel?

JAMB hasn't announced the 2027 Use of English reading text yet. The Lekki Headmaster by Kabir Alabi Garba was the set text for the 2025 and 2026 UTME. See our guide to studying The Lekki Headmaster while you wait for the announcement.

Last checked 23 November 2026. JAMB changes dates and rules from year to year. Always confirm on jamb.gov.ng.
